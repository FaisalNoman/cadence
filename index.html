Features

What Cadence actually does

Six things, all shipped, all verifiable by opening the file.

Accurate timers, every time

Cadence keeps time by the end timestamp, not by counting seconds. Even if your tab stays in the background, it drifts less than 1 second over an entire session.

Timers that work for you

Work cycles, short breaks, long breaks — choose the minutes that fit your rhythm. Set how many cycles trigger a long break and let Cadence auto-advance through your day.

Track your day

See at a glance how many focused sessions you've completed today. A session counter resets at local midnight, so your count always matches your actual day.

Keep your streak

A current and best streak, plus a 14-day inline history chart, keeps your momentum visible without ever leaving the page.

Your data, portable

Export your timers, streaks, and history as JSON. Save it, move it, reload it on another device. No accounts, no vendor lock-in — your data is yours to back up.

Keyboard shortcuts, full control

Space to start/pause. R to reset. S for settings. No mouse required. Cadence meets WCAG AA contrast standards in both light and dark themes.

Privacy & data

Your data, your control

Cadence makes zero network calls — you can verify this in your browser's developer tools; the Network tab stays empty.

Where is my data?

Your timer data (sessions, streaks, settings) is stored in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device.

What if I clear my browser data?

If you clear your browser's local storage, your history will be cleared. Before you clean up, export your data as JSON — one click. You can import it anytime to restore your sessions and streaks.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Click Export to download your data as a JSON file. You own it — keep copies, move it between devices, or import it into a fresh browser session at any time.

Does it sync across devices?

No. Cadence is deliberately device-local. Export your data on one device, download Cadence on another, and import to pick up where you left off.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Cadence free?

Yes. Download and use Cadence with no limits, no ads, no paid tiers.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your computer.

Does Cadence require an internet connection?

No. It works completely offline. Once you download the HTML file, you have everything you need.

What happens if I close my browser or turn off my computer?

Your streak and session data are saved in localStorage and will be there when you return. If you're worried, export your data regularly — it takes one click.

Can I change session lengths?

Yes. Open Settings (press S or click the gear icon) and adjust work, short break, and long break durations to whatever you prefer. Changes apply immediately to the next cycle.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. Press Space to start/pause the timer, R to reset, and S to open settings. No mouse required.

Does Cadence work on mobile?

It's a standard web page, so it loads in mobile browsers and buttons respond to touch. We haven't done systematic mobile testing yet, so we can't claim it's touch-optimized.

How accurate is the timer?

Cadence uses end-timestamp math rather than tick counting. It drifts less than 1 second, even if the tab runs in the background. This accuracy is verified by 167 automated tests.
